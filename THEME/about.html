About Vicinity

Your Community, Connected

Vicinity is a hyperlocal community platform that helps neighbors connect, share information, discover local businesses, and participate in community events — all in one place.

Version 2.4.0 Released September 2026

Our Mission

We believe that strong communities are built on connection. Vicinity was created to bridge the gap between neighbors who live on the same street but have never met, between local business owners and the customers who need them, and between community organizers and the volunteers who want to help.

Whether it's finding a trusted mechanic, joining a weekend running group, getting alerts about water supply interruptions, or organizing a neighborhood cleanup — Vicinity makes it effortless to stay informed and engaged with the place you call home.

Our Values

Trust First

Verified profiles, community moderation, and transparent reviews build trust between neighbors.

Hyperlocal Focus

Content is organized by LGA and neighborhood — you only see what's relevant to your area.

Community Owned

Groups are created and managed by residents. Vicinity provides the tools, you provide the community.

Safety & Privacy

Granular privacy controls, secure messaging, and proactive safety features protect every member.

Local Economy

Supporting neighborhood businesses keeps money circulating in the community.

Inclusive by Design

Multi-language support, accessibility features, and low-data mode ensure everyone can participate.

Key Features

Community Feed

Real-time posts from your neighbors — announcements, recommendations, lost & found, discussions.

Groups & Communities

Join or create groups for security watches, hobbies, markets, faith groups, and more.

Local Events

Discover and RSVP to neighborhood events — markets, festivals, workshops, cleanups, meetings.

Business Directory

Verified local businesses with reviews, hours, photos, and direct contact options.

Save for Later

Bookmark posts, businesses, and events to revisit anytime from your Saved page.

Smart Notifications

Customizable alerts for posts, events, security updates, and group activity.
